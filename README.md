DAA Blog – Recursion & Iteration
About
This blog is my way of breaking down two core ideas in algorithms: recursion and iteration. Instead of just definitions, I’ve tried to make it feel like a conversation — with examples, diagrams, and reflections that connect the concepts to real problem-solving.

What’s Inside
Blog Page (index.html) – the main write-up
Visuals – diagrams comparing recursion vs iteration, workflows, and complexity
Examples – Python snippets for factorial, array traversal, and more
Applications – where these techniques show up in real life (searching, sorting, etc.)

Why This Blog
To make algorithm concepts less intimidating
To show both the technical side and the human side of learning
To keep originality and integrity at the center of academic work
